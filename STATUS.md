# Playable arena status

The macOS application launches XARENA1 from the verified JP BIN/CUE. The player selects one of twelve vehicles and fights a computer-controlled opponent on the selected difficulty. The arena includes acceleration, reversing, steering, turbo, tire contact, bridges, collision damage, police pursuit and attacks, gun heat, missiles, pickups and regeneration. Health, selected ammunition and gun heat are visible in the chase view. Zero health costs one life. R restores the stage-entry checkpoint. CDDA and decoded VAB voices play through Bevy.

The player completed a manual keyboard playthrough, heard game sounds and reached the **Arena cleared** victory screen. Keyboard play is the accepted input configuration for this milestone.

Sweet Tooth, Yellow Jacket, Darkside, Hammerhead, Outlaw, Crimson Fury, Warthog, Mr. Grimm, Pit Viper, Thumper, Spectre and Road Kill
load their own driving profile, collision geometry, three damage models,
special weapon and audio programs. Each selection was launched on the
Apple M3 Pro Metal backend and its chase-view frame inspected. Arena draws its opponent from the original card order with the BIOS random sequence. New interactive contests sample the emulated root counter when preparing the draw. Deterministic captures use a counter value of 1. Restart restores the same draw and simulation seed. All six arenas support their opening encounters. Rooftops continues into the Minion encounter. Winning advances the single-player campaign. Defeating Minion opens the JP reward screen and scrolling credits. The title screen leads into a keyboard contest selector. The title offers the single-player contest, local two-player battle and main options.

On the duel first-pick screen, either keyboard layout can claim the first slot. Simultaneous input gives player two priority. The garage opens after both players release their mapped controls. While waiting, the menu plays the default screen engine (sound ID 0x52, shared with Crimson Fury) at rev 30. Entering the garage stops this motor and starts the selected car’s engine and signature. After both picks, the battle setup menus play player one’s motor at rev 30. The first-pick prompt times out after 1,200 JP video fields (20 seconds).

On the second pick, holding both horizontal directions consumes Select and leaves the displayed car in place. Vertical navigation has priority. On the first pick the JP shortcut records hidden card 12 in the showroom. A disc-backed replay executes the JP card mapping and player-car setter with the twelve registered CARS.DMD vehicles. The setter preserves the slot’s previous assignment. Immediately after uaInit these assignments are Yellow Jacket and vehicle ID zero. For zero the JP battle-options picture lookup returns index 12, one past the twelve filenames. This reads the first arrow record as a filename pointer. The keyboard shortcut hands the current card to the other player and hides the first portrait. Battle uses the previously assigned vehicle for the hidden slot, including after returning from a duel. A hidden pick by player one supplies Minion’s engine sound to the setup menu. If player two uses the shortcut with an unassigned slot, the port returns to the title when the other player confirms. This recovers from the original invalid picture lookup.

Single-player stage setup carries the deal sequence through card angles, pedestrian setup, player registration and opponent placement. After the opponents reserve their starting points, uaInitDB samples the counter. InitLevel initializes the helicopter where present, initializes the player again and samples the combat counter. An Arena 5 test runs both JP schedulers up to their counter reads with two pairs of seeds. It checks the identity and coordinates of all five rivals, the complete player record and the RNG after the second car initialization. The shell then consumes twelve BIOS random values for six windshield marks in both game modes. A new life samples a fresh combat seed and regenerates the marks. Keyboard restart restores the original stage combat seed and spawn layout. The host counter models the JP counter frequency and wraparound. Exact boot and disc-load timings are platform dependent.

The title, garage and both pause menus restart the navigation tone on repeated movement and the confirmation tone on repeated selection. UA_SOUND uses separate sound IDs 0x86 and 0x87 with request mode 5, so a confirmation can overlap a navigation tone. Disc-backed tests check that confirming in the duel audio menu replaces the confirmation voice, preserves the navigation voice and leaves both untouched on timeout. The opening movie skip also replaces an earlier confirmation.
