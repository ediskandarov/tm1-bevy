The STR assembler, BS bitstream decoder, quantization and zigzag tables, and IDCT basis in this directory are adapted from [PSoXide psx-fmv](https://github.com/ebonura/PSoXide/tree/6bfe478ff0e0c6e226cdf67b164df311c1658fec/sdk/crates/psx-fmv), licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. See COPYING. The host adaptation uses standard Rust little-endian reads and stores the expanded IDCT matrix. The upstream guest driver and SDK are not build dependencies.
