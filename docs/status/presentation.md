## Rival names and font sprites

## Opening sequence and cinema

A normal launch displays the three original JP startup TIMs and plays the Sony logo and title movies in order. The shell reads each STR as a bounded range of raw sectors on track 1. The built-in Rust STR decoder assembles video sectors, decodes BS frames and reconstructs 320 × 240 RGBA images. The Rust XA decoder supplies stereo PCM to Bevy at the native 37,800 Hz. The player reads video sectors from the movie extent on demand and drops its file reader on skip or completion. The original `MOVIE.c` lookahead leaves the last numbered frame undisplayed. Sound and image pause while the window is unfocused. Movies sample held Enter, Space, P or Cross, Start and Select on the first pad once per displayed movie frame. Skipping plays the menu confirmation at the configured effects volume. The Sony logo accepts input starting with lookahead frame 31. The final displayed frame bypasses the input callback, so natural completion is silent even with a confirmation button held.

After 1,201 idle fields the title cycles through the battle movie, the contest history, and the vehicle demonstration on successive visits. History scrolls the 31 lines of HISTORY.TXT over SCRNCALY.TIM. Natural completion introduces the twelve competitors, while Enter skips to the title movie. The vehicle demonstration uses SCRND2.TIM, rotates through the original card order at 361 fields per vehicle, and plays each signature over the title theme. A disc-backed integration test covers startup order, logo skip timing, title frame changes, the theme handoff and the idle demo. To inspect the title movie in a deterministic Metal run, use `--menu --demo-screenshot local/title-cinema.png 378`.
